Both files were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-24 and saved here for review. They are **concept art only**. Neither is a layered rig or an animation-ready letter asset.

- [`original-fairy-study.png`](original-fairy-study.png): transparent full-body character study. Prompt: “Create an original fairy character illustration for a cinematic website intro: one full-body, upright, narrow silhouette standing as the I in TINK. Warm brown skin, dark auburn loose hair with a tiny seed-pearl pin, expressive thoughtful face, elegant cream-and-moss embroidered long dress, slender translucent amber moth-like wings with delicately inked veins. Museum-quality watercolor-and-graphite storybook illustration with exquisite hand-drawn detail, forest green, warm ivory, muted antique gold and sepia. Curious, charming expression. Entire figure centered, no clipping, generous transparent padding. Transparent background. No text or scenery. Entirely original: do not resemble Disney Tinker Bell; no blonde bun, green petal mini-dress or blue wings.”
- [`tink-intro-keyframe.png`](tink-intro-keyframe.png): flattened visual keyframe. Prompt: “Use the original fairy as identity and style reference, preserving her dark auburn hair, warm brown skin, cream-and-moss dress, amber moth wings and face. Show the exact uppercase word TINK, immediately legible, with T, N and K formed from entwined tree branches and engraved botanical stems; the fairy stands as the I, with no separate I. Deep matte forest-green woodland background, restrained gold light and sparse ivory motes. Old storybook engraving blended with luxury botanical packaging. No neon, particle explosion, Disney likeness, extra text, logo or watermark.”

The keyframe uses [`original-fairy-study.png`](original-fairy-study.png) as its reference image. Its letters and background are baked into one picture; see [`../INTRO_ANIMATION.md`](../INTRO_ANIMATION.md) for the production layers needed to animate it.
